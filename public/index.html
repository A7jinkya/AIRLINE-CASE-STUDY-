About this app

An interactive dashboard over the Airline Dataset Updated – v2: passenger-flight records from , covering departure airports in countries.

How it works

  • The dashboard filters a pre-aggregated data cube in your browser, so every chart updates instantly.
  • The Passengers tab calls a serverless endpoint, /api/passengers, to search and filter all records.

Read this before drawing conclusions

  • Flight status is almost exactly one-third On Time, Delayed and Cancelled, and shows no meaningful relationship with continent, gender, age or month. The data appears to be synthetic, so differences between segments are random noise.
  • Departure dates arrive in two formats. Dates like 06-10-2022 are ambiguous and were read as day-first.
  • A value of 0 in Arrival Airport was treated as missing.
